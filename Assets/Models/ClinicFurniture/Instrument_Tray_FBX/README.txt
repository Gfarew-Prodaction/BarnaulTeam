FOUR-COMPARTMENT MEDICAL INSTRUMENT TRAY

Approximate reference dimensions: 0.35 x 0.28 x 0.035 metres.
One closed, manifold render mesh; 3,780 triangles; 7 simple collision boxes that preserve the open cavities.
UV0 material coordinates; UV1 unique lightmap unwrap.
Four actual recessed compartments; tapered walls, rounded inner corners, smooth bottom transitions and formed outer edge.
2K BaseColor/Roughness/Normal maps and a Metallic map; no lighting or reflections baked into the textures.
All textures embedded in FBX and supplied separately.
Normal maps: OpenGL tangent space (+Y).
FBX excludes cameras, lights and studio ground.
PBR_Materials.json lists channel assignments.
