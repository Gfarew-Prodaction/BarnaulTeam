AGAU COMPLETE ROOM - FBX

14 render meshes / 24,052 triangles; 9 simple UCX collision meshes.
No cameras, lights, animation or hidden source lettering.
Clear interior: 5 x 5 x 3.2 metres. FBX axes: -Z forward, Y up; unit scale: metres.
UV0: material; UV1: lightmap.
Textures are embedded in FBX and also supplied in Textures/.
PBR_Materials.json maps each material to its colour, normal, roughness and metallic maps.
Normal maps: tangent-space OpenGL (+Y). Import normal files as normal maps; roughness and metallic as linear data.
FBX material conversion differs by engine: assign the PBR maps according to PBR_Materials.json if needed.
Door leaf pivot is at its hinge. UCX_* meshes are collision only.
